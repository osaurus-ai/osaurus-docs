# Quick Start

This page takes you from a fresh install to your first chat in about five minutes. You'll install the app, choose how your AI runs, meet your team, and run your first workflow. All you need is a Mac with an M-series chip and macOS 15.5 or later.

## 1. Install

1. Download Osaurus from [osaurus.ai](https://osaurus.ai/).
2. Open the download and drag **Osaurus** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open Osaurus. A quick way: press `⌘ Space`, type "Osaurus", and press Return.

Want to chat offline the moment it opens? There's also a larger **full** download (about 4 GB) that includes the Raptor 0.7 model, so you can skip the model download. Full guide: [Installation](/installation#pick-a-build).

## 2. Choose how your AI runs

The first time you open Osaurus, a small welcome window appears, and the Osaurus dino says hello.

1. Click **Get started**. The switch below it, **Share anonymous usage data**, is optional. It never includes your chats, files or keys.
2. Osaurus asks **What matters most to you?** Pick one. You can change it any time later.
   - **Keep it private, on this Mac** (recommended): downloads **Raptor 0.7**, a [model](/glossary#model) that runs on your Mac. It's free, works offline, and nothing you type leaves your Mac. The download is about 3.7 GB, and you can start while it finishes.
   - **Start chatting right away**: uses [Osaurus Cloud](/glossary#osaurus-cloud). Nothing to download, with free credit to start.
   - **Use an AI account you already have**: sign in with your ChatGPT, Grok or OpenRouter account, use Claude Code, or paste a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and others.
3. Follow the next screen, then click **Continue to Osaurus**.

Not sure what something means? Click the dino on any screen and it explains that step.

**Skip setup**, at the top right, starts you on Osaurus Cloud straight away and skips the introduction. You can add a local model later in **Settings → Local Models**.

If you installed the full download and Raptor 0.7 fits your Mac, Osaurus skips this choice and uses it straight away.

## 3. Meet your team

Your first chat opens with a short hello from Osaurus, followed by your team. Each [teammate](/glossary#teammate) is an agent that looks after one part of your day:

| Teammate | Looks after |
|---|---|
| **Planner** | Your Calendar and Reminders |
| **Courier** | Your Mail. It never sends anything without asking. |
| **Keeper** | Your Downloads folder (it only reads it) and Notes |
| **Scout** | Research on the web |
| **Builder** | One code project folder. Only on Macs set up for coding. |

1. Pick a teammate and try one of its tasks, or type your own, like "help with my mail".
2. The first time a teammate needs one of your apps, macOS asks for permission. Click **Allow**.
3. When it's done, click **Meet Another Teammate** to try someone else, or **Done** to finish.

You talk to **Osaurus**, the [Orchestrator](/glossary#orchestrator). It hands the job to the right teammate and brings back the answer, so you don't have to pick one yourself.

To skip the introduction, click **Skip setup** at the top of it, or type "skip".

Later, new chats may show **Set Up Osaurus**, followed by how many things are left, under the greeting. Click it to see what's left to try, like turning on a workflow or pairing your phone. Click **Skip setup** beside it to stop the suggestions.

## 4. Run your first workflow

A [workflow](/glossary#workflow) is a task Osaurus remembers how to do. Your team came with seven. Try **Morning Brief**:

1. Click **Workflows**, the second icon on the narrow strip along the left edge of the window.
2. Find **Morning Brief** and click **Run**.
3. A moment later you have one page with today's events, what's due, and the mail that needs a reply. It all happened on your Mac.

Like it? Have it ready every morning:

1. Click **Settings** (the gear at the bottom of the same strip), then **Automations**.
2. Click **Add Automation**, choose **Schedule**, and set it to daily at 8:00.
3. Under **Do**, choose **Run Workflow**, then **Morning Brief**, and click **Save**.

Or just ask Osaurus in a chat: "Run Morning Brief every weekday at 8." [Automations →](/automations)

## 5. Make one and share it

Got a task you do every week? Describe it, and Osaurus builds the workflow for you.

1. On the Workflows page, click **New Workflow**.
2. Type what you want, like "Every Friday, write a short update about this week's meetings."
3. Answer its questions, click **Test Run**, and when you like the result, click **Save…**.

To share it, click **Share…**, choose **Publish to store.osaurus.ai**, set **Who can see it** to **Unlisted**, and click **Publish**. Copy the link and send it to anyone. They can install it on their own Mac, and your keys never leave yours.

[Build with the Architect →](/workflows-build) · [Share a Workflow →](/workflows-share)

## Open a chat from anywhere

1. Press **`⌘;`** from anywhere on your Mac. The chat window appears.
2. Type something, like: *What's on my calendar this afternoon?*
3. Press Return. The reply appears as it's written.
4. Press `⌘;` again to hide the window.

## Have it work on your files

Agents can do real work, not just answer. Try this:

1. Press `⌘;` to open a chat.
2. Click the folder button on the message box and pick a folder you don't mind it changing. This becomes the chat's [Working Folder](/glossary#working-folder).
3. Ask: *"Summarize what's in this folder and add a README.md describing it."*

You'll see the work tick off as it reads files and writes the new one. The new file shows up as a card in the chat. Every change is recorded, so you can review or undo it in the chat's **File Changes** panel. [Working in Chat →](/agent-loop)

## Try voice

Click the microphone in the message box and speak. Your voice is turned into text on your Mac and never leaves it. You can also set a shortcut key to dictate into any app on your Mac. [Voice →](/voice)

## Under the hood

- **Signing:** the app is Developer ID signed and notarized, so it opens without Gatekeeper warnings.
- **Onboarding steps:** Welcome → **What matters most to you?** (Configure AI) → the first-run walkthrough in chat. The only local model onboarding offers is Raptor 0.7 4B (`OsaurusAI/Raptor-0.7-4B-JANG_6M`), on every Mac; add others from Settings → Local Models. With the full build, Configure AI is skipped when the bundled model is installed and fits comfortably.
- **Skip setup** (or closing the welcome window with no model chosen) starts on Osaurus Cloud, skips the walkthrough, and still creates the team.
- **The team and workflows** are created on fresh installs only. Builder appears only when Xcode, the command-line tools, or a git configuration is present. Updates from Osaurus Classic get neither; **Create starter agents** in Settings → Agents makes the team. See [Upgrading](/upgrading).
- **Custom endpoints:** **Custom** accepts any OpenAI-compatible server URL. Local endpoints don't need a key.
- **Core Model:** on macOS 26+ the [Core Model](/glossary#core-model) (used for memory and tool picking) defaults to `foundation` (Apple Intelligence). On macOS 15, pick a small local or cheap cloud model in **Settings → Models → Model Defaults → Core Model**, or [memory](/memory) pauses.
- **Osaurus ID:** your identity key is created on setup and lives in your iCloud Keychain, gated by Face ID or Touch ID. Save your 24-word [recovery phrase](/glossary#recovery-phrase) from **Settings → Osaurus ID** when you have a minute. See [Osaurus ID](/identity).
- **Sandbox:** on Macs that run the sandbox VM, its runtime downloads quietly in the background after onboarding and starts the first time an agent runs a command. It's a Linux VM on macOS 26+ and a Seatbelt-confined runner on macOS 15.
- **Later choices:** privacy settings are in Settings → Privacy & Security; credits are in Settings → Osaurus ID → Wallet & Credits; native plugins are in Settings → Tools & MCP → Plugins.
- **Voice:** transcription runs on-device on Apple's Neural Engine.
